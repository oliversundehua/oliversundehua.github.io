Welcome!

I am an Assistant Instructional Professor of Political Science in the Master of Arts Program in the Social Sciences (MAPSS) at the University of Chicago. My research focuses on political socialization, political psychology, and public opinion in China.

I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of Rochester and my B.S. in Mathematics from Lafayette College.

Contact: sund@uchicago.edu
